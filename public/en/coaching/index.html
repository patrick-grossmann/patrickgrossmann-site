Who are you today, really?

You have achieved a lot in your career. And yet something no longer fits: the role feels wrong, the energy is gone, or you are standing at a crossroads. Often that is not about you, but about a self-image that has not grown with you.

What you walk away with

  • Your why.You know what makes you who you are and what is worth getting up for, even on a rainy Tuesday morning.
  • An up-to-date self-image.You know which parts still hold and what you can let go of.
  • A clear target picture.You know where you want to go and what you will deliberately stop doing.
  • A plan for everyday life.Priorities and routines that hold up under pressure.

Questions people bring to me

  • What do I really stand for in my work?
  • Which parts of this are me, and which are just other people's expectations?
  • Where am I still acting from old patterns instead of clear conviction?
  • How do I make good decisions when the pressure is high?
  • What is my north star for this next phase?

Five steps. You decide after each one.

You do not have to commit to a big programme. Every step delivers a result you keep.

  1. 01

    Inner Clarity Review

    20 minutes · free

    We talk about where you stand and what is on your mind. Afterwards you know whether coaching with me makes sense for you. No sales pitch.

  2. 02

    Foundation: Know your why.

    3 sessions of 60 min · fixed price €549

    We sort out which parts of your self-image still hold and which are just habit.

    Result: your personal why profile, in writing. Values, strengths, inner drivers and your own definition of success.

  3. 03

    Blueprint

    2 sessions · optional 360° feedback

    Your why becomes a target picture: where do you want to go, and what will you deliberately let go of? If you like, we gather the view of three to five people around you. They often see strengths you overlook yourself.

    Result: your target picture and a 90-day plan.

  4. 04

    Integration

    4 sessions over about 8 weeks

    We bring the target picture into your everyday life: priorities, decisions, routines. Between sessions you put things into practice. In the sessions we look at what holds.

    Result: you act on your plan, not on old habits.

  5. 05

    Refresh

    optional · cancel monthly

    Two short check-ins a month, so your update does not go out of date again.

Steps 2 to 4 take about 90 days in total. The easiest way in is the Foundation: a fixed price and a result you keep, even if you stop there.

Frequently asked questions

What does the coaching cost?

You pay per phase, not per hour. Foundation: €549. Blueprint including 360° feedback: €399. Integration: €749. Refresh: €189 a month for two 30-minute check-ins, cancel monthly; unused sessions expire. You can book additional sessions at any time: €199 for 60 minutes. All prices are final prices. No VAT is charged under the German small business rule (§ 19 UStG).

Do I have to book all five steps?

No. Every step has its own result. After the Foundation you have your why profile, whether or not you continue.

What happens in the Inner Clarity Review?

We talk for 20 minutes about where you stand and what you want. You get a first impression of how I work, and I get an impression of what you need. If it is not a fit, I will tell you honestly.

How does the 360° feedback work?

You choose three to five people who know you well. They answer a few short questions, anonymously if they prefer. We go through the answers together in the Blueprint. It is optional.

Online or in person?

All sessions take place online via video. That way the coaching fits into your life wherever you are.

In English or German?

Both. At least half of my coaching is in English.

How is this different from therapy?

Coaching looks ahead: at clarity, decisions and putting things into practice. For mental illness or acute crises, therapy is the right path.

Is everything confidential?

Yes. Even if your employer pays, what we talk about stays between us.

Time for your update?

Take 20 minutes. Together we look at where you stand and whether the next step with me is the right one.
